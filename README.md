# Simple Interest Calculator

This calculator computes the interest earned or owed on a principal amount at a fixed annual rate over a period measured in years.

## Inputs

- **Principal (P)** — the initial amount of money.
- **Annual interest rate (R)** — the percentage rate per year. Enter `5` for 5%.
- **Time (T)** — the number of years. Enter `0.5` for six months.

## Formula

`Simple interest = (P × R × T) / 100`

The result is the interest in the same currency as the principal. To find the total amount, add the interest to the principal.

## Example

For a principal of $1,000 at 5% per year for 2 years, the simple interest is `(1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = $100`. The total amount is $1,100.
